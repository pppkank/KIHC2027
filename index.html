About the conference

One Health for a resilient Kedah, and beyond

Health risks now move between people, animals and the environment. KIHC 2027 brings every sector that shapes them into one room.

Climate change, extreme heat, pollution, zoonotic diseases, antimicrobial resistance, disasters and the growing burden of non-communicable diseases cannot be solved by the health sector alone. Kedah's mix of urban centres, rice-growing land, coastal and island communities and an international border makes it a natural testing ground for practical, cross-sector solutions.

Building on the inaugural KIHC 2025, this second edition widens the lens from community engagement to One Health, planetary health, sustainability and resilience. Over two days, policymakers, practitioners, researchers, local authorities, industry and communities will share evidence and leave with commitments they can act on.

  • 1Strengthen inter-agency collaboration in applying the One Health approach.
  • 2Share knowledge and innovation on communicable and non-communicable diseases, AMR, climate change and environmental health.
  • 3Improve health-system preparedness and resilience across human, animal and environmental health.
  • 4Build strategic partnerships and joint action for sustainable community wellbeing.
